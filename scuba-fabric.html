What Scuba Fabric is

What It is Characterized By

Scuba fabric is a special type of knitted material that is characterized by a smooth surface, high hand feel, and inbuilt elasticity. The material has soft foamy feel, but the composition is lightweight which allows it to be worn over a long period of time.

It was designed to create clean, architectural lines, and is capable of four-way stretch, as well as long-life durability and great dimensional stability. In DG Knitting, we manufacture scuba cloth using high-quality polyester based yarns in order to achieve consistency in performance and high aesthetic value.
