Our Journey

Weaving Success Since 1998

DG Knitting began with a simple vision: to create premium knitted fabrics that combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology. Over two decades later, we've grown into a trusted partner for India's leading fashion brands, garment manufacturers, and textile exporters.

Our journey from a small knitting unit to a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is driven by our commitment to quality, consistency, and innovation. Today, we operate advanced machinery across three specialized knitting domains — Warp, Circular, and Flat knitting — serving diverse industries from fashion apparel to technical textiles.
