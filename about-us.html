Our Journey · Registered 11/07/2017

Where Every Thread Carries a Promise

DG Knitting was built on a simple belief — that in a world obsessed with scale, the most powerful currency is still trust. From the very first sample we sent a client, to the tonnes of orders that followed, every relationship we have ever built has been personal, intentional, and lasting.

With a ₹45 crore turnover rooted entirely within India, we haven't chased the world. We've earned our home market — stitch by stitch, partner by partner. Since our registration on 11th July 2017, we have grown into a state-of-the-art facility operating advanced machinery across warp, circular, and flat knitting, serving India's leading fashion brands and garment manufacturers.
