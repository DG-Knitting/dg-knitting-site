Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide fabric samples?

Yes, we provide fabric samples for all our products. Please fill out the contact form or email us at sales@dgknitting.com with your requirements.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our MOQ varies by product. Typically, it's 500 kg per style. However, we can discuss smaller quantities for trial orders.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery typically takes 7-14 days depending on your location and order quantity. We'll provide exact timelines when you place your order.

Do you export internationally?

Currently we serve the Indian market, but we're planning to start exports in 2025-26. For international inquiries, please contact us for future opportunities.
