Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Can I see samples before ordering?

Absolutely. We believe fabric should be felt, not just described. Let us know what you're looking for, and we'll send samples your way.

Do you work with small quantities?

Every long partnership starts with a first step. While our standard MOQ is 500 kg, we're happy to discuss trial quantities for new relationships.

What's your typical turnaround?

Most orders ship within 7-14 days. Once we understand your timeline, we'll work backwards to make sure you're never waiting.

Do you work with international clients?

Currently we're focused on serving India with excellence. We're preparing for global expansion in 2025-26 — stay connected with us for updates.
