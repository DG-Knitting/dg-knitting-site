What We Test, Why It Matters

Quality Parameters

GSM Consistency

Uniform weight isn't just a number — it's the foundation of predictable garment making. We ensure every meter feels the same, performs the same.

Standard: ±3% variation

Color Fastness

Colors that fade tell the wrong story. We test rigorously so that every shade you choose remains vibrant — through sunlight, sweat, and multiple washes.

Standard: Grade 4+

Pilling Resistance

Fabric should age gracefully, not show wear after a few uses. Our pilling tests ensure the surface stays smooth, the feel stays premium.

Standard: Grade 3-4

Tensile Strength

Strength isn't just about endurance — it's about reliability. We measure how much force fabric can take, because your production line deserves zero surprises.

Standard: ASTM D5034

pH Balance

Fabric touches skin, and skin deserves care. We ensure our fabrics are pH-neutral, safe for even the most sensitive applications.

Standard: 4.0-7.5
