What We Test

Quality Parameters

GSM Consistency

Ensuring uniform weight across the fabric for predictable performance and accurate costing.

Standard: ±3% variation

Shrinkage Control

Testing and controlling shrinkage to ensure garment dimensions remain stable after washing.

Standard: < 5% shrinkage

Color Fastness

Ensuring colors remain vibrant and don't bleed or fade through multiple washes.

Standard: Grade 4+

Pilling Resistance

Testing fabric resistance to pilling formation for long-lasting aesthetic appeal.

Standard: Grade 3-4

Tensile Strength

Measuring fabric strength to ensure durability during garment construction and use.

Standard: ASTM D5034

pH Balance

Ensuring fabric pH is skin-friendly and within safe limits for all applications.

Standard: 4.0-7.5
